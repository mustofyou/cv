About me

What I do and what I care about.

I work in data and machine learning: exploring datasets, training models, and wrapping them in simple web apps so others can try them. My notebooks range from classic exercises to clustering and prediction projects.

I'm especially curious about language models, AI agents, NLP and spatial data. I also keep my own notes on cloud services such as Google Cloud and AWS, written in both English and Turkish. This site's night-sky theme is my nod to a lifelong habit of looking up.
